Good to know

The honest notes

Local AI is slower than the cloud

On an ordinary laptop, a long report takes a minute or two. Add a cloud key for speed, and keep local AI for private things.

Made by one developer

There's no 24/7 support team. Every email is read by the person who built the app, and requests from real users get built first.

The installer isn't code-signed yet

Windows shows a "Run anyway" warning. The SHA-256 checksum on the download page lets you check the file is genuine.

No phone apps yet

iPhone and Android apps are on the way. The browser extension works today in Chrome, Edge, Brave and Opera.

    Download

    Windows

    Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit

    1. Run ClavexisAI-…-windows-setup.exe.
    2. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click More info → Run anyway.
    3. Click Next → Install. No admin password needed.
    4. Create your account. The first account on a computer is its owner.
    Download for Windows

    macOS

    macOS 12 or newer, Apple silicon or Intel

    1. Install Python 3.12 from python.org.
    2. In Terminal, in your Downloads folder:
      tar -xzf ClavexisAI-*-macos.tar.gz && cd ClavexisAI
    3. Start it with ./clavexisai.sh. The first start takes 5-10 minutes.
    Download for macOS

    Linux

    Ubuntu 22.04+, Debian 12+, Fedora 39+

    1. Install Python:
      sudo apt install python3 python3-venv python3-pip
    2. Unpack: tar -xzf ClavexisAI-*-linux.tar.gz
    3. Start it with ./clavexisai.sh. Add --install for an app-menu entry.
    Download for Linux

    Quick start

    Your first 10 minutes

    1. Install (2 minutes)

      Windows: run the installer. If Windows warns you, click More info → Run anyway, then Next → Install. Mac / Linux: unpack the download and run ./clavexisai.sh.

    2. Create your account (1 minute)

      The first account on a computer is its owner. Pick a strong password, and turn on two-step login later in account settings.

    3. Activate your licence (30 seconds)

      On the lock screen, paste the key from your email or your account and press Activate.

    4. Pick your AI (3 minutes)

      Install Ollama and run ollama pull llama3.2:3b for free, private AI. Or add a cloud key in Settings → AI & Model → Cloud AI keys and press Test.

    5. Try five things (3 minutes)

      • Ask anything in Chat. The answer says which model wrote it.
      • Attach a photo of a receipt and ask about it.
      • With a photo attached, type "remove the background".
      • On Windows, press Ctrl+Alt+D in any app and talk.
      • Ask for research on any topic, and check the numbered sources.
    Choosing your AI

    Local, cloud, or both

    Every answer is labelled with the model that wrote it and whether it ran on your computer or in the cloud, so you always know where your words went.

    Adding a cloud key

    Open Settings → AI & Model → Cloud AI keys, pick the provider, paste the key and press Test. It works straight away, with no restart. Supported: OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, OpenRouter, DeepSeek, Mistral, Groq and xAI.

    Photo readers

    With local AI, a small photo reader (SmolVLM2, about 10 seconds a photo) describes the picture for the chat model. Press 🔍 Look closer for the bigger reader (Gemma 3 4B, 1.5-2 minutes) when detail matters. With a cloud model, the photo goes to the model directly.

    Think harder

    Turn on 🧠 Think harder in the composer for hard questions. Answers are slower and more careful.

    Everyday tips

    Getting more out of it

    • Find anything you've talked about with the search box in the top bar. Results show the matching words and open at that message.
    • Ask about earlier chats. "What did you tell me last time about…" uses your past chats.
    • Private one-off questions go in a temporary chat, which isn't saved.
    • Study mode (🎓) turns it into a tutor that asks you questions instead of giving answers away.
    • 🎧 Listen on any answer makes a two-host audio overview with your computer's own voices.
    • ⚖ Compare sends the same question to another model, side by side.
    • 🗒 Canvas opens an answer or code beside the chat. Ask the AI to change all of it, or select a part first.
    • Rename a chat by clicking its name in the bar above the conversation. Type the new name and press Enter.
    • The Code assistant carries on from your last coding session and sees the file you have open, so you can ask "why does this fail?" without pasting code.
    • Ask about your day in Briefing. It knows your tasks, today's plan and the weather: try "what should I do first?".
    • Find a setting by pressing Ctrl+F in Settings (⌘F on a Mac), or click Find.
    • Make it yours in Settings → Appearance: themes, a random theme that changes each time you start or with your mood, your own mouse pointer, and tips that say what a button does when you point at it.
    • Connect your calendar with its private iCal link (ends in .ics), then ask "am I free Friday?".
    • Gmail needs an app password, not your normal one: Google Account → Security → 2-Step Verification → App passwords.
    • Laptops: plug in the charger for local AI. On battery, Windows slows the processor down.
    Licence and computers

    Your licence

    • Activate: open ClavexisAI, paste the key when it asks (or Settings → Account → Licence) and press Activate. Your key is in your account and in the email we sent.
    • 3 computers: one licence covers up to 3 of your own computers.
    • New computer: remove the old one from the licence on your account page, then activate on the new one.

    • Sharing: a licence is for you (or your organisation) only. Shared or republished keys can be turned off.
    Privacy and your data

    What stays where

    The ClavexisAI app runs on your computer. Your chats, photos, files, email, calendars, memory and settings stay there, and we never receive them. If you choose a cloud provider, your messages go from your computer straight to that provider under its own privacy policy.

    Where your data is stored

    SystemFolder
    Windows%LOCALAPPDATA%\ClavexisAI
    macOS~/Library/Application Support/ClavexisAI

    What the store keeps

    Your account (name, email, a salted password hash), orders and invoices, licence activations (key, anonymous device ID, device name, app version) and early access emails. Order and invoice records are kept 8 years for tax law; activation logs while the licence is active and one year after.

    Deleting your data

    In the app, delete chats or your whole account. For store data, email contact@clavexis.com. One essential cookie keeps you signed in on the store, and there are no advertising or tracking cookies.
