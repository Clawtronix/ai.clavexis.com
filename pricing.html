Questions

Can I try it first?

There's no free trial. The tour on the home page shows every screen of the real app, and if ClavexisAI doesn't work on your computer as described and we can't fix it, you get your money back.

How do I pay?

At checkout you pay the exact amount by UPI (scan the QR code with any UPI app) or by bank transfer, then enter the transaction reference. We confirm the payment and your licence key appears in your account and inbox — usually within a few business hours.

How do I use my licence?

Open ClavexisAI and paste the key when it asks (or Settings → Account → Licence) and press Activate. A licence covers up to 3 of your own computers; you can move a key to a new computer from your account page.

Does monthly renew automatically?

No. You pay for one month up front. Buying another month extends your existing key — nothing to reinstall.

What does lifetime include?

One payment, no end date: everything in ClavexisAI version 1 and all its updates, on up to 3 of your computers.

Not to us. Use free local models (Ollama) on your own computer, or plug in your own key for OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, OpenRouter, DeepSeek, Mistral, Groq or xAI, and pay that provider directly for what you use.

No. A licence is for you or your organisation only. Under the licence agreement you sign at checkout, republishing or giving the software or key to anyone else makes you liable for the price of every copy provided, plus compensation.
